# <span class="kind-enum">RoadSignType</span> enum

</div>

<div class="section desc markdown">

A road sign type classifying road signs that can appear along a road.

Some signs are standardized and look the same in all countries, e.g. <a href="sdk-for-flutter-navigate-navigation-roadsigntype">RoadSignType.stopSign</a>. In general, the visual appearance of the road signs can differ across countries. Some road signs can be combined with other signs, like `WeatherType` signs. The road sign will be always shown topmost.

</div>
