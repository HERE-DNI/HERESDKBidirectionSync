<h1>RoadSignType enum</h1>
</div>

<p>A road sign type classifying road signs that can appear along a road.</p>
<p>Some signs are standardized and look the same in all countries, e.g. <a href="sdk-for-flutter-navigate-navigation-roadsigntype">RoadSignType.stopSign</a>.

Some road signs can be combined with other signs, like <code>WeatherType</code> signs. The road sign will be always shown topmost.</p>
