<div class="block">

Time period when the tariff element is valid, in local time. The time
period wraps around to the next day, when end time of the period to is
smaller than the beginning from . Note: This is a beta release of this
feature, so there could be a few bugs and unexpected behaviors. Related
APIs may change for new releases without a deprecation process.
