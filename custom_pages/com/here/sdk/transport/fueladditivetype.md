### AUS32

public static final [FuelAdditiveType](sdk-for-android-explore-api-reference-latestfueladditivetype "enum class in com.here.sdk.transport") AUS32

    A fuel additive. AUS 32 (aqueous urea solution) is used as an additive to the fuel in diesel vehicles with SCR (Selective Catalytic Reduction) technology to reduce the NOx emission. AU 32 is also known as ARLA 32 or HWL.
