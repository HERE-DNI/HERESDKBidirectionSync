    ### origin

    </div>

    <div class="block">

    The origin specifies the top-left corner of the rectangle. When this
    point is used to indicate the coordinates on a view, then (0,0) will
    mark the top-left corner of the view. The size determines the width
    and height of the rectangle. The width expands towards the right of
    the view. The height expands towards the bottom of the view.

    </div>
