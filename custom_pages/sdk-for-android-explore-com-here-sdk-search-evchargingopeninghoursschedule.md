<div class="block">

Opening hours schedule for EV charging locations, represented by a list
of days of the week during which the location is open in the given time
periods. Note: This is a beta release of this feature, so there could be
a few bugs and unexpected behaviors. Related APIs may change for new
releases without a deprecation process.
