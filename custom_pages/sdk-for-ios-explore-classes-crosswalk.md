</div>

Represents crosswalk’s inside the <a href="sdk-for-ios-explore-classes-venuelevel">`VenueLevel`</a>. A crosswalk is an area of the road surface where pedestrians are expected to walk across the road. The area is represented as a polygon, which is often, but not necessarily, rectangular and oriented with the shorter dimension in the vehicle’s direction of travel.
