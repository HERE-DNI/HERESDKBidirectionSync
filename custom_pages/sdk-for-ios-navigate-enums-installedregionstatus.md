  Download is not finished yet. Either try to download this region again and, eventually, the progress will continue where it was left off - or you can cancel this download or delete this region. The following reasons can lead to this status: An ongoing or paused download or an abrupt end, for example, when the app was closed. Ongoing or paused downloads can be resumed with the <a href="sdk-for-ios-navigate-classes-mapdownloadertask">`MapDownloaderTask`</a>.
