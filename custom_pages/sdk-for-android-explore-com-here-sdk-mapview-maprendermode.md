    <div class="member-signature">

    <span class="modifiers">public static

    <div class="block">

    Map rendering will use TextureView . This method has a performance
    penalty, but makes MapView behave like a regular view, supporting
    view transformations and alpha and avoiding graphical glitches that
    SurfaceView can suffer from in certain scenarios.
