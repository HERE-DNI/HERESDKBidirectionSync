  <div class="abstract">

  This event is issued when a better route could be found, as defined by <a href="sdk-for-ios-navigate-structs-dynamicroutingengineoptions">`DynamicRoutingEngineOptions`</a>. To find a better route, two routes are calculated. The updated current route: A route that is calculated via the route specified. The dynamic route: A route that starts at the current position on the route specified and passes through the remaining waypoints.

  </div>
